FLUJO 1: LISTAR EMPLEADOS
   User clicks "Visualización"
   ↓
   /employees loads
   ↓
   EmployeesList fetches GET /employees/?skip=0&limit=100
   ↓
   Table renders with search
   ↓
   User searches by name/ID/email
   ↓
   Click row → Navigate to /employees/{id}

FLUJO 2: VER DETALLE
   /employees/{id} loads
   ↓
   EmployeeDetail fetches GET /employees/{id}
   ↓
   Personal info card renders
   ↓
   User selects date
   ↓
   Click "Ver Día" o "Ver Timeline"

   /employees/{id}/day/{date} loads
   ↓
   DayView fetches:
     • GET /employees/{id}
     • GET /attendance/reports/daily/...
   ↓
   Grid de métricas renders
   ↓
   Click "Ver Timeline" para más detalles

FLUJO 4: TIMELINE DETALLADO
   /employees/{id}/timeline/{date} loads
   ↓
   TimelineView fetches:
     • GET /employees/{id}

   ↓

   ↓
   Shows timeline with anomalies & recommendations
